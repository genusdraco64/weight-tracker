# Wellness and Medical Disclaimer

Weight Tracker is an informational wellness logging and analytics tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, prevent, or cure any condition.

Nutrition values, exercise calories, trends, projections, and coaching statements may be estimates. They are not substitutes for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Seek qualified medical care for health concerns. Seek immediate emergency assistance for symptoms such as chest pain, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, or other urgent warning signs.
